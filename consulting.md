<p class="lead">
I help research groups, planning offices, mobility companies, and infrastructure stakeholders apply pedestrian dynamics simulation in a scientifically sound, reproducible, and practical way.
</p>

<p>Services include simulation audits, JuPedSim consulting, training workshops, technical prototyping, and expert review of pedestrian and evacuation simulation workflows.</p>

<p>One person to talk to, with a network of specialists I bring in when a project needs more. I handle the core myself.</p>

<h2>Services</h2>

<dl>
  <dt>Pedestrian Simulation Audit</dt>
  <dd>Independent expert review of simulation scenarios, assumptions, model choice, input data, calibration, reproducibility, and interpretation of results.</dd>

  <dt>JuPedSim Consulting</dt>
  <dd>Support for JuPedSim-based workflows, Python scripting, scenario setup, analysis, visualization, and integration.</dd>

  <dt>Training &amp; Workshops</dt>
  <dd>Hands-on workshops for JuPedSim, pedestrian dynamics, evacuation simulation, and reproducible simulation workflows.</dd>

  <dt>Simulation Tool Prototyping</dt>
  <dd>Development of web-based research prototypes, interactive simulation tools, and analysis dashboards.</dd>
</dl>

<h2>Process</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Discovery call</strong> — a short conversation to understand the question, the constraints, and what a useful outcome looks like.</li>
  <li><strong>Scoping</strong> — a written proposal with deliverables, assumptions, data needs, timeline, and a fixed price.</li>
  <li><strong>Engagement</strong> — audit, workshop, or prototype sprint, with checkpoints along the way.</li>
  <li><strong>Handover</strong> — documented results, reproducible workflows, and a debrief on what the simulation does and does not support.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Demos</h2>
<p>Short walkthroughs of the JuPedSim App — a free, browser-based interface I maintain for pedestrian crowd simulation. It runs the full <a href="https://www.jupedsim.org">JuPedSim</a> pipeline in a browser tab with no installation: sketch or import geometry (DXF from CAD, IFC from BIM), define agents and exits, run the simulation, and export results for analysis in Python.</p>
