<h2>Process</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Discovery call</strong> — a short conversation to understand the question, the constraints, and what a useful outcome looks like.</li>
  <li><strong>Scoping</strong> — a written proposal with deliverables, assumptions, data needs, timeline, and a fixed price.</li>
  <li><strong>Engagement</strong> — audit, workshop, or prototype sprint, with checkpoints along the way.</li>
  <li><strong>Handover</strong> — documented results, reproducible workflows, and a debrief on what the simulation does and does not support.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Demos</h2>
<p>Short walkthroughs of the JuPedSim App — a free, browser-based interface I maintain for pedestrian crowd simulation. It runs the full <a href="https://www.jupedsim.org">JuPedSim</a> pipeline in a browser tab with no installation: sketch or import geometry (DXF from CAD, IFC from BIM), define agents and exits, run the simulation, and export results for analysis in Python.</p>
