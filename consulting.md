# Scientific Consulting for Pedestrian Dynamics and Simulation Software

I help research groups, planning offices, mobility companies, and infrastructure stakeholders apply pedestrian dynamics simulation in a scientifically sound, reproducible, and practical way.

Services include simulation audits, JuPedSim consulting, training workshops, technical prototyping, and expert review of pedestrian and evacuation simulation workflows.

---

## Services

### Pedestrian Simulation Audit
Independent expert review of pedestrian simulation scenarios, assumptions, model choice, input data, calibration, reproducibility, and interpretation of results.

### JuPedSim Consulting
Support for JuPedSim-based workflows, Python scripting, scenario setup, analysis, visualization, and integration.

### Training & Workshops
Hands-on workshops for JuPedSim, pedestrian dynamics, evacuation simulation, and reproducible simulation workflows.

### Simulation Tool Prototyping
Development of web-based research prototypes, interactive simulation tools, and analysis dashboards.
