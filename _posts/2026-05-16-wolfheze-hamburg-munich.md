Last week was an intensely refreshing one.

It started in Wolfheze with a CROWDING project meeting — socio-psychologists, computer scientists, mathematicians and physicists in one room. Creativity and original ideas were all over the place. In the evening we all watched the Champions League semifinals together — though some left a bit sadder after Bayern's exit ^^.

Big things coming. And more strength to open-source software.
